I am Ines Okafor. I trained as a biologist, worked as a ranger in the Bavarian Forest for six years and now photograph the animals I used to count.

Most of my work happens within a day's drive of home, before breakfast, from a hide or a hole in the ground. I do not bait, I do not use playback calls, and I do not publish the location of a nest.

> A good picture of an animal shows that it had no idea you were there.

## Prints and workshops

Every photograph on this site is available as a signed print in three sizes. Twice a year I take four people into a hide for a weekend; the dates are on the [Frames](/tags/frames.html) page. For both, write to [ines@example.com](mailto:ines@example.com).

## In the bag

- Two mirrorless bodies, 45 MP, silent shutter
- The long one: 600 mm f/4
- The honest one: 35 mm f/1.8
- A one-litre thermos, non-negotiable
