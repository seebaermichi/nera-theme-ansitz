A post without a picture, because the list is the point.

## The list

- A second battery for every hour of darkness, in an inside pocket
- A sleeping bag rated ten degrees colder than the forecast
- A one-litre thermos, non-negotiable
- A cushion for the bench and a blanket for the slit
